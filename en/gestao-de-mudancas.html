Change Management

What is it?

A complete methodology for managing change across projects of varying complexity. It covers readiness assessment, impact management, communication, training, stakeholder management and knowledge management.

What is it for?

  • Identify, diagnose and manage resistance, preparing the organization to absorb change resulting from projects or process shifts.

Why is it good?

  • Being a complete methodology, it translates the intangible aspects of change acceptance into objective, manageable criteria.
  • Through knowledge transfer, the company doesn't just manage the current change — it learns to live with the ones that follow.

How does it work?

  • A knowledge-transfer process.
  • Typically applied during a project or a portfolio of projects.
  • Based on a defined set of processes and tools.
  • Built alongside the client's team throughout the engagement.
