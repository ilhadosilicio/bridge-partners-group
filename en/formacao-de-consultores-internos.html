Internal Consultant Development

What is it?

A program focused on developing internal professionals to act as an in-house consulting unit within the organization itself.

What is it for?

  • Enable the setup of support and business-facing teams that work more assertively, optimizing relationships.
  • Turn a cost center into a results center, increasing visibility of the team's efforts.

Why is it good?

  • Becomes an excellent vehicle for applying the company's strategy and governance, framing support-area work around a service-delivery mindset.
  • Provides greater visibility into return on investment for the whole organization.

How does it work?

  • A knowledge-transfer process.
  • Starts with a one-week workshop to build the strategy.
  • The strategy is broken down into a set of processes guided by governance.
  • Based on BPG's reference model, templates are created for use going forward.
  • A client area is identified as the pilot for rollout.
