Career Management

What is it?

A career planning and management program based on the CARMA methodology (Career and Relationship Management).

What is it for?

  • Establish a clear, unified approach to career planning, creating an efficient communication protocol between professionals and the company.
  • Strengthen professionals' bond with the company, increasing retention of critical talent.

Why is it good?

  • When professionals take ownership of managing their own careers, the company gains a clearer view of the team and people feel more motivated.
  • Increases transparency in communication between company and employee, boosting retention.

How does it work?

  • A full strategic career-planning program.
  • Participants receive guidance through a workshop, a program handbook, and the tools needed to build their plan.
  • They get ongoing mentoring support while executing their plans.
