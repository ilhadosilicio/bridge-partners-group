Since 1999

Respect for the client's context and culture

In 1999, we founded Bridge Partners Group with the goal of being the strategic consulting arm of the Presstexto Comunicação group. That's when we began our dream of building a consulting firm different from the rest.

The relationships between the firm, clients and teams were increasingly focused on billing hours for this or that client, and less and less on the value generated by those relationships.

We reshaped our concept of career by abolishing org charts, and turned knowledge into tools — and that's how Bridge Partners Group was born. Our professionals form a group of people who are happy and fulfilled in their work.

Mission

To help corporations achieve the best possible return and sustained value from their projects, with a full focus on results and human capital.

Vision

We are the integrating element between our network of partners and clients, identifying and implementing solutions across the value chain.
